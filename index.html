About

From Biology to Scientific AI.

I began my career in experimental cancer biology, studying genes involved in nasopharyngeal cancer. I later pursued a PhD in computational biology at the Institute of Molecular Pathology in Vienna, researching gene regulation and genomics.

Over time, I transitioned toward data engineering and applied AI, combining my scientific background with software engineering to develop computational solutions for life sciences.

Today, I build AI systems and critically evaluate emerging AI technologies, particularly their reliability, limitations, and scientific usefulness.
